- Programming Concept:
```Summing 1D List```

- Code:
```total = 0
lst = [2, 4, 6, 8]
for i in range(len(lst)):
    total += lst[i]```

- Analogy to animate:
```Adding Up Coins in a Piggy Bank.
Imagine you have a small piggy bank and a row of coins on your desk: a 2¢ coin, a 4¢ coin, a 6¢ coin, and an 8¢ coin.
You want to know how much money you have in total, so you pick up one coin at a time and drop it into the piggy bank, keeping track of the total as you go.

**Mapping the Metaphor to the Code:**

- `total = 0`:
 This is like starting with an empty piggy bank — you have no money yet.

- `lst = [2, 4, 6, 8]`:
 These are your coins lined up on the desk — each number represents the value of a coin.

- `for i in range(len(lst)):`
 This loop is like you picking up each coin, one by one, from the leftmost to the rightmost.

- `total += lst[i]`:
 As you pick up each coin, you drop it into the piggy bank and add its value to your total savings.

**Example:**
You pick up the 2¢ coin → total is now 2¢
Then the 4¢ coin → total becomes 6¢
Then the 6¢ coin → total becomes 12¢
Finally, the 8¢ coin → total is now 20¢

So in the end, `total` holds the final value of all coins combined — just like in the code, where the total is the sum of the list.```
